## 8. AI AND AUTOMATED DATA PROCESSING

### Automated Systems Used

BFlow uses artificial intelligence and automated systems:

- **AWS Textract**: Automatically extracts text from photos of receipts and invoices. It helps categorize and organize transactions.
- **AWS Rekognition**: Analyzes images for inappropriate content (NSFW detection) to maintain security.
- **Automated Categorization**: ML algorithms categorize transactions based on merchant data.
- **Predictive Analysis**: Provides spending insights and financial trends.

### Accuracy and Responsibility

**Important:** Automated systems may make errors. You are solely responsible for:
